# Continue from durable work

Read the current frozen task, source and host feedback before relying on saved notes. Then inspect the source files, experiments, citations and unresolved questions referenced by the latest research summary. Saved notes are advisory; exact source and current check results take precedence when they disagree.

Preserve meaningful attempts as reproducible artifacts. A concise handoff should identify established declarations and assumptions, source/check references, unsuccessful routes and why they failed, outstanding questions and the most useful next checks. Keep a pointer to full artifacts when a summary omits detail; avoid repeatedly copying a large history into every message.

Use the provided knowledge search and import facilities for historical results. Read their exact declarations and conditions and allow the host to recheck them in the new environment. Historical acceptance records retain their original meaning; do not relabel them as fresh checks. Cross-task reuse must respect the run's source and knowledge-access rules.

Save work at natural milestones, before substantial route changes and when preparing a delivery or stopping. A normal turn boundary does not mean the mathematical responsibility has changed. Continue the same problem while it remains assigned; observe the user's actual resource and stopping policy rather than inventing an expiry or automatic problem rotation.
